# Post types

Ziply's standard post templates, the same ones Content Factory offers, per channel. Pick the type that fits the idea; it sets the post's shape, its call to action and the visual to suggest. Ziply's templates default to no emoji and a few relevant hashtags (never a block of them). The user's request and the brand's voice still come first.

Visuals aren't created or uploaded here: name the one the type suggests in the hand-over.

## Facebook

Moderate brand presence; warmer and more community-minded than LinkedIn. Saved with a type: Post, Reel or Story.

Community story · How-to (numbered steps) · Poll or question · Product or service highlight (benefits as a story) · Event promotion (details list) · Behind the scenes · Tip or value post (list) · Milestone or celebration · Seasonal or holiday · Contest or giveaway (rules stated in full) · Customer spotlight (real customers only) · Weekly roundup · Live video announcement · Photo album story · Fundraiser or cause · Local community event · FAQ (Q&A) · Throwback or anniversary (then vs now) · Customer appreciation.

## Instagram

Moderate brand presence; caption up to 2,200 characters, no clickable link (point to the link in bio). Saved with a type: Post, Reel or Story. Describe the image, carousel or video it needs.

Educational carousel (one point per slide) · Quote or inspiration · Product or service showcase · Behind the scenes · Customer content feature (with permission) · Tutorial reel (step by step) · Before and after · Poll or question story · Tip carousel · Lifestyle · Giveaway (rules stated in full) · Meet the team · Customer spotlight reel · Trend participation (adapted to the brand) · Product line showcase · Day-in-the-life reel · Testimonial highlight (real quotes only) · Making-of · Product styling · Series post (part X of Y).

## Reddit

Subtle brand presence, and always under the Reddit skill's rules: affiliation stated, communities the team belongs to, never the same text twice.

| Type | Shape |
|---|---|
| Ask Me Anything | Who's answering and their proof, then the topic |
| Discussion Starter | A genuine question with several perspectives |
| Question Seeking Advice | Context, what's been tried, the specific ask |
| Resource / Tool Share | What it is and why it helps, with the affiliation stated |
| Show and Tell / Project | What was built and how, with a TL;DR |

## YouTube

No post templates: write the title (up to 100 characters) and description (up to 5,000) from the transcript or notes the user supplies.
